## DonWeb DNS

The domain stays on DonWeb nameservers. Do not move the nameservers to Vercel
unless the whole DNS zone is intentionally moved.

Keep these DNS records:

| type | name | content | TTL |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A` | `julianmirigoyen.online` | `76.76.21.21` | `14400` |
| `CNAME` | `www.julianmirigoyen.online` | `cname.vercel-dns-0.com` | `14400` |
| `NS` | `julianmirigoyen.online` | `ns1.donweb.com` | `14400` |
| `NS` | `julianmirigoyen.online` | `ns2.donweb.com` | `14400` |
| `SOA` | `julianmirigoyen.online` | DonWeb default | `14400` |

Remove any duplicate `www` CNAME, especially a row that points
`www.julianmirigoyen.online` back to `julianmirigoyen.online`. There should be
one `www` CNAME.

## Analytics

Vercel Web Analytics was added to the app immediately before deployment.

Implementation:

- `@vercel/analytics` is installed in `package.json`.
- `app/layout.tsx` imports `Analytics as VercelAnalytics` from
  `@vercel/analytics/next`.
- `app/layout.tsx` renders `<VercelAnalytics />` inside `<body>`, after
  `{children}`.

There is also a separate local analytics wrapper:

- `app/analytics/Analytics.tsx`
- Mounted as `<Analytics />` in `app/layout.tsx`.
- It is env-gated and does nothing unless `NEXT_PUBLIC_CLARITY_ID` or
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_GTAG_ID` is set.

If the Vercel dashboard does not show data, check that Web Analytics is enabled
for the Vercel project, then redeploy. Vercel documents that the analytics
routes are added after enabling analytics and deploying.

Reference:

- https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/quickstart
- https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/troubleshooting

## Opening Section

The opening section in `app/scroll/Timeline.tsx` was changed around the user
request: "I love a good challenge should begin appearing when I start scrolling
down."

Current behavior:

- The old headline, `Eight years, every size of company.`, is gone.
- The headline is now `I love a good challenge.`.
- The headline uses a code-style mono face through `.jx-code-title`.
- The headline is plain text with `data-sc-kinetic="chars"`, so Scrollcraft
  splits it into characters and reveals it over scroll.
- The headline cue is `data-sc-cue="0 0.92 0.48 0.10"`.
- The body copy cue is `data-sc-cue="0.18 1 0.26 0.08"`, so it fades in while
  the title is still resolving.
- The open act is intentionally long: `data-sc-span="7"` and
  `data-sc-dwell="0.55"`.
- The model/video has a subtle float from `jx-open-float` in `app/theme.css`.
- The label says `Backend · Full-stack · DevOps · Agentic AI`.
- `agentive` was replaced with `agentic`.

## Scrollcraft Engine Notes

`public/scrollcraft/scrollcraft.js` is still vendored, but this build carries a
local compatibility edit:

- `data-sc-kinetic="chars"` now wraps characters by word inside
  `.sc-split-word`.
- `app/scrollcraft.css` defines `.sc-split-word` as an inline-block with
  `white-space: nowrap`.

That change prevents the code-font headline from breaking in the middle of a
word during character reveal.
